The Answer to All the World's Woes

by

A. T. Adams

November 10, 1997

The Problem:

The world never quite seems to understand the intent of The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™. If they did, humanity would clearly all just get along -- in perfect harmony. For thousands of years, people have been contemplating just why this is the case. Some have come up with possible conclusions, but none have ever provided an answer that could fully explain all contingencies. Fortunately, I have found it*: The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ has a speech impediment.

*Why is this? See below.

The Answer:

Develop and sell a translation device that corrects for speech impediments by breaking down commandments from The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ to the lowest possible language denominator - the morpheme - then re-assembling the intent to an unambiguous, Open Document Format for all the world to see. It's just that simple!

What is this device? Why nothing less than the Completely Organic, Automated, Electronic, Potentially Multi-Galactic MORPHEMETRON Professional (MP).

I have had an epiphany.

People, as I understand it, are frequently having these things, epiphanies that is, most especially when they get to be my age, and mine came while mowing the back lawn one November 10th in just that area where the the compost-bin meets the woodpile.

Since that time, I have thought about the underlying concepts within this epiphany a great deal. I have looked at the grand history of mankind, and, in considering it all in a balanced and impartial way, I believe my conclusion, which I am about to unfold, holds.

My epiphany is simply this; sadly, The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ has a speech impediment.

If one looks back and considers the last ten thousand years of recorded history, one finds that this sad fact is the best possible explanation for the suffering of humanity.

As societies have risen and fallen, hundreds, perhaps thousands, of people have clearly stated that they knew what The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ was thinking. However, only some of them ever seem to get it right. Even at that, they seem to get it right only some of the time.

Some individuals might say that this difficulty is due to the commonly held belief that The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ works in mysterious ways. As it turns out, the mysterious ways in which he works are pretty much this, The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ wants whoever hears him to kill everybody else. Even a brief introduction to the history of humanity will make this fact clear. People who believe they understand the will of The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ are forever killing other people who are equally certain that they are the ones who understand the will of The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™. It is due to the inexorably tenuous nature of monopolizing truth, I think, that all the real trouble begins.

Let's look at an example.

Some have said that it is the intention of the The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ that no one should kill anyone else, but clearly this interpretation is flawed, because, eventually, the majority of these people are killed off by the ones who heard it the other way.

Here are some examples of the types of confusion created by garbled messages from The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™.

What they Think They Heard -- Commentary Don't kill anyone. (A nice, but ultimately short-lived interpretation, truly held by very, very few (that is, when taking the long-view throughout history - clearly there are case-by-case examples where individuals have managed this feat without issue)). Don't kill anyone who looks just like you. (Popular, but ultimately and historically, impractical). Don't kill anyone who looks just like you except for in cases when they have something that you particularly want. (The historical evidence for this interpretation is so overwhelming as to be thoroughly obvious).

As a side observation, I must here note that now-a-days people who purport to hear what the The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ is saying are generally given Lithium and placed in resorts whose names typically include the words Mental Health Center. However, long ago, people were more intellectually evolved in these matters than we are today. In the past, people who said they could hear The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ talking to them were often held in great esteem. Some of them seem to have sorted out the whole speech impediment thing and compensated for it, for a while. On the whole though, most of these people were eventually killed by other people where were generally ticked-off at the fact that the person who was hearing the words of The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ didn't look like just what they looked like.

In an ideal world, it seems clear, everyone would wish for someone who understands the The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™, despite the speech impediment, and looked pretty much like what they look like.

On a side note I need mention polytheists here. On the whole, polytheistic ideologies have difficulties of their own owing to the combined problems of compensating for speech impediments from multiple deities as well as continually sorting out which Invisible Deity in the Sky outranked any other Invisible Deity in the sky for any specific area of expertise. While such complicated menageries have the benefit of providing ample fodder for, typically adulterous, conjecture for their general goings on, it is probably the case that, more often than not, such confusion led most people prefer the tacit simplicity of a single, unified incomprehensible being, for overall convenience, in a handy, pocket-sized word.

Atheists needn't be mentioned at any length as their gig requires that the greatest majority of the human race is completely batty. Though they do seem to get to the same stage as most other organizations of the same genre, ie. Having a monopoly on the truth, but without the whole hassle of having to compensate for any speech impediments from any higher powers. (Please Note: Speech impediments involving any specific atheists, in particular, are beyond the scope of this document).

Now, to be fair, it is worth mentioning here that still others have stated that they believe there is no Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™. There is instead, they counter, an Invisible Woman in the Sky (who controls the universe)™. I have given this concept a great deal of thought also, and it too goes a long way toward explaining things.

I say so from personal experience. During my time on Earth, despite my occupation of being something of a computer expert, I have, in fact, actually met a woman on more than one occasion.

On some of these occasions, I have spoken with a woman, or even, sometimes, more than one. When I have been particularly fortunate, I have even spoken with more than one at the same time.

I find that, on the whole, their theory also holds water -- especially in light of the fact that I have believed myself to have understood what it is these women have said and have later found myself to have been entirely wrong.

As close as I can determine, my difficulty is caused by some mysterious entities which these women call Phea Lings.

Now, in the interest of science, I have attempted to acquire some idea of just what these Phea Lings are. Perhaps I have done so out of season, or the lighting just wasn't right, or I had woken up too early or looked too late, but I have never actually managed to see the things, myself, and am personally convinced that they do not, in fact, exist.

Back to the point, this Invisible Woman in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ concept is an interesting study, and I'd warrant that perhaps all the confusion throughout time has simply been caused by the fact that it has always been an Invisible Woman in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ -- which would be an extraordinarily easy mistake to make on account of the whole invisibility factor.

However, historically, there have been similar ratios of both men and women who thought they had understood what The Invisible Woman in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ had said and were just as wrong. (citation needed).

Which of course begs the question of whether there is simply an Invisible Person in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ who has a speech impediment -- regardless of gender.

I find this answer the most likely. Thus, from this point forward, because we humans are so apt at providing genders for things, and in an effort to avoid any confusion, I shall here forward randomly assign genders to The Invisible Person in the Sky (who controls the universe)™.

Now, one may ask, as I have, if it has always been true that the The Invisible Woman in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ has always had a speech impediment, why doesn't she simply fix it and thereby utterly resolve the confusion

There are several possible explanations for this. Number one, The Invisible Woman in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ knows all about the speech impediment thing, but is not, in fact, omnipotent, and, consequently, can't remedy this sad difficulty. Throughout history there has been a great deal of conjecture in this area, and the consensus has been that this is not, in fact, the case. Number two, partially out of politeness, but mostly out of the fear of how the observation might be taken by one so unthinkably powerful, no one has ever told The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ that he has a speech impediment. I think that this might explain things, but given the number of people who have purported to have spoken with The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™, I find it unlikely that absolutely no one has ever mentioned it before. Thirdly, and I need mention the option I find to be most likely, people have told The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ about the speech impediment, but The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ simply doesn't listen to what people say.

So, there you have it, the answer that explains the tumultuous history of human kind when no other theory could.

It's a simple matter that, sadly, cannot be corrected. (Or couldn't be, UNTIL NOW!) (link).

Now, I could go on about this, but for a sinking fear of generally ticking-off The Invisible Woman in the Sky (who controls the universe)™. I have observed the way The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ has treated people who have tried their darnedest to do what they think he said - bestowing upon them pestilence, disease, starvation, and, more recently, blowing them into tiny bits.

If The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™ treats devote followers in this manner, I cower to think how he might regard one who has figured out the whole mess. Perhaps horrendous things might befall me, or, on the other hand, wondrously uplifting things might occur. Without any objective method of determining the intents of The Invisible Man in the Sky (who controls the universe)™, there is simply no way to be certain.
